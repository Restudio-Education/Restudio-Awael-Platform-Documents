What is the Al-Awael platform?

Al-Awael Platform is a platform developed for Al-Awael Intermediate School in Abha, Saudi Arabia. (The platform was created by Restudio Education)

Are there any Restudio products on the AL-Awael Platform?

The Jane's Office suite includes a range of productivity applications, such as Restudio Presentation, Restudio Tables, and Restudio Documents. Designed with simplicity and efficiency in mind, the suite offers a lightweight alternative to Microsoft 365 while providing essential tools for creating documents, presentations, and spreadsheets.

For more information, please visit the official Restudio website.

Does AL-Awael Platform rely on Microsoft 365?

Yes, but only partly because AL-Awael Platform relies on Restudio Janeoffice.

Is Jane's the best alternative to Microsoft 365?

Yes, Restudio JaneOffice serves as a lightweight and user-friendly alternative to Microsoft 365, particularly for users seeking simplicity and efficiency. However, for organizations or individuals requiring a comprehensive productivity ecosystem with advanced features and extensive cloud integration, Microsoft 365 remains the more capable and feature-rich solution.

If I encounter a problem with AL-Awael Platform, can I contact Restudio Support?

Yes! You can explain the problem you encountered with the platform to Restudio Support or contact the Al-Awael School's technical support manager.
